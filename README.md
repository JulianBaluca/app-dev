*Spider-Man: No Way Home* is a superhero movie about Peter Parker, who asks Doctor Strange for help after his identity as Spider-Man is revealed to the world.

- The action scenes are amazing.
- It teaches the importance of friendship and responsibility.
- It brings together different Spider-Man characters.
